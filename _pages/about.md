I am currently a Research Scientist Intern at Mistral AI on the Multimodal team, working on Vision Encoder and multimodal models. Upon completion, I will earn a Master’s degree in Mathematics, Vision and Learning (MVA) from ENS Paris-Saclay and graduate from École nationale des ponts et chaussées, where I specialized in Mathematics and Computer Science.


I have fairly broad research interests, among them **VLM pre-training**, **Representation Learning** (JEPA, SSL for videos, ...), **World Models**, **flow matching** and **reasoning**. I conducted research on flow matching at Harvard AI and Robotics Lab, supervised by Prof. <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=i9B02k4AAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" style="color: inherit;">Mengyu Wang</a>. I have also worked on Large Language Models during an internship at ChapsVision, more precisely about training for agentic/tool calling abilities. 

I am the first author of [Delta Rectified Flow Sampling for Text-to-Image Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05342), accepted at CVPR 2026. I also co-authored [SplitFlow: Flow Decomposition for Inversion-Free Text-to-Image Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25970), accepted at NeurIPS 2025. Thanks to Minghan Li, Sung-Hoon Yoon, Jaeyon Kim and Mengyu Wang for their incredible collaboration. I also co-authored [PAGE-4D: Disentangled Pose and Geometry Estimation for 4D Perception](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2510.17568), accepted at ICLR 2026, with <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=8S6_34oAAAAJ&view_op=list_works" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" style="color: inherit;">Kaichen Zhou</a>. I also contributed to [Mistral Large 4](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/).

You can find my code and projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/gaspardbd).
